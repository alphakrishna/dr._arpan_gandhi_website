Laboratory Operations Consulting

A high-performing laboratory is built on far more than advanced equipment and technology. It requires robust systems, efficient workflows, empowered teams, financial discipline, and an unwavering commitment to quality.

The Practice

With nearly three decades of experience in diagnostic laboratory leadership, I work with hospitals, diagnostic centres, healthcare organizations, and investors to transform laboratory operations into efficient, scalable, and clinically impactful services.

My consulting approach combines medical expertise with operational excellence. Whether supporting the establishment of a new laboratory, improving an existing facility, or guiding organizational expansion, I focus on creating sustainable systems that enhance quality, improve turnaround times, optimize costs, strengthen governance, and deliver measurable business outcomes.

Philosophy

Consulting Philosophy

Every laboratory is unique. Rather than applying generic solutions, I work closely with leadership teams to understand clinical priorities, operational challenges, financial objectives, and organizational culture. The goal is not simply to solve today's problems, but to build resilient laboratory systems that continue to perform as the organization grows.

My role is to help healthcare organizations bridge the gap between clinical excellence and operational efficiency — creating laboratories that are trusted by clinicians, valued by patients, and positioned for long-term success.

Typical Engagements

How I Typically Engage

  • Designing new pathology and molecular laboratories
  • Improving turnaround time and workflow efficiency
  • Strengthening laboratory governance and quality systems
  • Preparing laboratories for accreditation
  • Developing expansion strategies for diagnostic networks
  • Advising healthcare investors on laboratory projects
  • Supporting hospital leadership with diagnostic transformation initiatives
  • Mentoring laboratory leadership teams and future medical directors
